# Sprint 43 validation — member support recovery

Sprint 43 adds a protected member support surface at `/app/support`, a member-owned support request lifecycle, duplicate-safe request identity, observed-version withdrawal/reopen recovery, factual member-only projections, and provider-neutral in-app support status notifications. Existing staff support remains permission-scoped and now rejects staff withdrawal of member requests, verifies active assignment targets, protects updates with observed timestamps, and notifies the member without exposing staff notes, safety evidence, private conversations, identity documents, or payment credentials. The support schema changes are additive: requester ownership, withdrawn state, and indexing were applied through migrations `0032_loud_lady_bullseye.sql` and `0033_cloudy_outlaw_kid.sql`.

Focused Sprint 43 coverage passed: **20 tests across 4 files**, including support ownership/idempotency, stale member recovery, staff permission boundaries, privacy-minimized projections, notification policy, offline-safe UX, and Premium neutrality. The complete release gate passed: **480 tests across 98 files**, TypeScript validation, production build, and `pnpm audit --prod --audit-level=high` with no known vulnerabilities.

Read-only visual review covered desktop and 375px mobile routes: `/app/support`, `/app/account`, `/app/safety`, `/app/verification`, `/app/billing`, `/app`, and `/admin/support`. The preview account has no submitted support request, so the review shows truthful empty/recovery states rather than real member cases or staff activity. No request, assignment, status change, notification delivery, provider, external communication, account mutation, or launch state was created or claimed.
