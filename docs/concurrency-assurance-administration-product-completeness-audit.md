## Verified checkpoint additions

The checkpoint adds request fingerprinting with a server-only HMAC, keyed duplicate/conflict outcomes, privacy-safe conversation event metadata, and concurrency tests for same-key text/voice, network-interruption retry, different keys, and same-key conflicting payload. It also standardizes high-value active administration surface states across verification, Trust & Safety, connection readiness, editorial/photo review, country operations, approvals, incidents, finance, and support.

## Remaining internal priority

The highest remaining internal product polish is progressive shared-state adoption in the remaining less-frequently used administration modules, plus focused usability refinement of data-dense staff tables once controlled fictional staff accounts and representative queue data are available. Neither item authorizes a new provider, policy, safety action, or external infrastructure change.
