## Required onboarding checklist

Access may be considered only after all items are complete and recorded through the approved workflow:

1. Confirm appointment, scope of work, and accountable approver.
2. Execute written confidentiality and security obligations.
3. Record intellectual-property and code-ownership terms.
4. Define least-privilege access and non-production boundary.
5. Create a separate developer account with strong authentication.
6. Define access expiry, review, and immediate revocation procedure.
7. Enable audit logging and incident reporting.
8. Obtain explicit approval for any production-related activity.

The developer must never receive shared credentials. Secrets remain in the approved manager and are not placed in chat, documentation, repository files, or environment-variable exports. Access to member information and identity documents is outside the default scope and requires a separate documented decision.
