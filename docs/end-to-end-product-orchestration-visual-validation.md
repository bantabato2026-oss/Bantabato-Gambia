Existing source contracts provide reduced-motion and low-bandwidth suppression evidence. Multi-account, screen-reader, keyboard, microphone, invitation, staff, media, and dependent state-change browser scenarios remain **BLOCKED — EXTERNAL ACTION REQUIRED** until the prepared fictional identities can be authorized in an isolated environment.
