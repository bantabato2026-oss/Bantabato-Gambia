# BANTABATO — Major Product Capability Sprint 5 Visual Validation

## Scope and method

This is a **read-only presentation review** of `/app/verification`, `/app/family`, and `/app/international` at 1280 × 720 and 375 × 812. No document was selected or uploaded; no invitation, resend, revoke, permission change, acknowledgement, feedback, location preference, notification, account, provider, or browser permission was executed.

| Route | Desktop review | Mobile review | Observed result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/app/verification` | The entry state clearly distinguishes private manual review from a verification badge and shows document requirements. | The current unavailable/retry recovery state is single-column, legible, and provides a clear retry action. | Read-only recovery/entry presentation is coherent; no upload or status transition was claimed. |
| `/app/family` | The recovery state was observed when private family data was unavailable. | The empty Family Circle, Parent/Wali/Guardian invite form, explanatory boundary panel, and large submit control remain readable at 375px. | The layout preserves simple language and explicit private-boundary explanation. No invitation was created. |
| `/app/international` | The core-profile prerequisite state is concise and preserves privacy-first country/diaspora context. | The prerequisite card and its profile continuation action remain readable without horizontal overflow. | No country, long-distance preference, discovery refresh, or recommendation refresh was executed. |

## Findings and limits

The verification entry surface and Family Circle invitation form use clear labels, visible actions, and natural single-column mobile layout. The available preview alternated between safe prerequisite/recovery states; it did not provide an authorized fictional multi-member environment for observing private document lifecycle, invitation acceptance, participant sharing, preference saving, or discovery changes.

> This review does **not** prove document upload, reviewer authorization, signed-document access, invitation replay/expiry, permission revocation, feedback visibility, eligibility refresh, notification delivery, keyboard-only interaction, formal screen-reader behavior, low-bandwidth transitions, or external-provider behavior. Those remain subject to authorized fictional-account or hardware-assisted validation.

## Boundaries retained

No public document path, precise location, country score, Family Circle access to messages/documents/safety/billing, provider capability, real account, or launch-readiness claim was introduced or observed.
