## Verified public pricing

TiDB Cloud Starter is publicly listed at **$0/month to start**. The published free allowance is 25 GiB row storage, 25 GiB columnar storage, and 250M Request Units per organization across up to five eligible instances. Published rates beyond the allowance start at $0.10 per million Request Units, $0.20 per GiB-month row storage, and $0.05 per GiB-month columnar storage. Region, provider, storage, backup, data transfer, and usage can change the amount.

The Starter details state that each eligible instance can receive 5 GiB row storage, 5 GiB columnar storage, and 50M Request Units monthly. Scalable instances require a spending limit and may require a payment method; limits can throttle or deny new connections.
