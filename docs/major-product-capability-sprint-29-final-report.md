# BANTABATO — Major Product Capability Sprint 29

## Session Security, Data Rights & Account Control

Sprint 29 adds an additive member security-session ledger that records only a hash of a real authenticated session reference, status, bounded timestamps, and revocation evidence. It stores no raw token, cookie, device, browser, IP address, or location. The centralized authentication path now observes the actual session hash, preserves the JWT expiry, and rejects revoked or expired records before protected routes run. The Account Center provides a member-owned observed-session list with current, active, revoked, and expired states; individual and bulk other-session revocation; a current-session sign-out boundary; in-app security updates; transaction locking; stale observed-version checks; duplicate-safe behavior; and fresh-sign-in protection for revocation and data-rights actions.

Account lifecycle and data-rights controls now have clearer factual boundaries. Pause is temporary and keeps saved profile information private. Reactivation still re-runs existing eligibility rules and cannot restore a safety restriction or bypass consent. A deletion review request removes discovery and recommendations, withdraws current connections and related Family Circle sharing, and remains a request rather than a deletion claim. Existing messages, voice media, membership, notifications, and safely retained operational or safety records are not represented as deleted. Data Rights Center presents member-owned data categories, excluded categories, and requested, processing, ready, expired, cancelled, unavailable, and failed states without fabricating a downloadable file.

Privacy controls remain profile-scoped, server-confirmed, stale-safe, offline-disabled, and subordinate to safety, verification, eligibility, blocks, restrictions, and consent. Family Circle does not gain access to messages, documents, safety records, account controls, or call consent. Security, privacy, data-rights, and availability actions do not receive Premium advantage.

Focused unit and source-contract coverage includes session usability, cross-member ownership, current-session preservation, stale/duplicate/concurrent revocation, fresh authentication, central enforcement, protected data boundaries, request lifecycle, deletion consequences, offline handling, notification privacy, accessibility confirmations, and premium neutrality. Complete validation passed: **408 tests across 84 files**, TypeScript, production build, and production dependency audit. Desktop and 375px mobile reviews used an existing authenticated preview route without invoking pause, reactivation, revocation, privacy change, request, cancellation, sign-out, or other account action. The review confirmed the observed-session hierarchy, profileless privacy recovery, server-state labels, read-only data summary, destructive-action separation, and responsive one-column layout.

## Product classification

Bantabato now has real server-enforced member session revocation for sessions it has actually observed, plus a clearer member account and data-rights boundary. It remains **NOT READY**: an authorized deletion-retention and final-deletion policy, a generated and authorized data-export delivery workflow, long-term session/device inventory policy, a defined account-recovery channel, and consented member usability and accessibility evaluation are genuine remaining product gaps.
