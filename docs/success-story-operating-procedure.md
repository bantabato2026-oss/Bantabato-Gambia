# BANTABATO — SUCCESS-STORY OPERATING PROCEDURE & INTERNAL GAP CLOSURE

**Status:** Approved internal operating procedure  
**Effective scope:** Voluntary member success-story declarations and any possible public presentation.  
**Non-goal:** This procedure does not make a marriage claim, a matchmaking guarantee, a testimonial, a rating, or evidence that a relationship is successful.

> **Core rule:** No public success story may bypass the full consent, editorial review, independent publication approval, publication, and withdrawal lifecycle. A private declaration is never public by default.

## Authoritative lifecycle

| Stage | Required state | Who may act | Control and record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Member declaration | `draft` or `private` | The declaring member | The member records engaged or married privately. Future-contact consent is separate from public-story consent. |
| Voluntary public-story consent | `pending_review` | The declaring member | The member supplies a short optional story, explicitly requests editorial review, records public-name preference, and separately authorizes any selected approved photo. |
| Fresh authentication | `pending_review` submission gate | The declaring member | A recent server-observed sign-in is required before a voluntary public-story submission. A stale session must sign out and sign back in; this is not a substitute for consent. |
| Editorial review | `approved` or `rejected` | Permission-scoped reviewer | Review consent completeness, accuracy, privacy exposure, contact details, sensitive information, safety concerns, language, cultural appropriateness, and presentation. Reviewers see no private messages, family information, verification documents, exact location, or internal safety content. |
| Independent publication approval | Approved operational record | Separate authorized approver | The publication requester and approver must be different staff identities. Fresh reauthentication and an audit event are required. |
| Public presentation | `published` | Permission-scoped publisher | Only the approved story summary, optional authorized display name, and any separately authorized future public-media derivative may be presented. Never show private conversations, contact details, family information, verification material, internal safety data, private location, or an unapproved image. |
| Withdrawal | `withdrawn` | The declaring member | Immediately remove the story from normal public discovery and revoke display-name and photo authorization while preserving the compliance/audit record. Republish requires a new voluntary submission and the entire lifecycle again. |

## Consent and photograph rules

Member consent is **specific, affirmative, and revocable**. It is not bundled with account terms, profile privacy, contact permission, or normal profile-photo visibility. The member must understand that an accepted story could be publicly presented, that a separately selected approved photo might only be considered for public use if explicitly authorized, that editorial changes may be made for clarity and presentation, and that withdrawal stops normal presentation.

Normal private profile-photo visibility never grants editorial rights. A public-story photo decision requires a selected approved photo, a separate affirmative authorization, an authorization timestamp, the member identity, a success-story editorial purpose, and revocation on withdrawal. The current public presentation surface is text-only; no original private media path is exposed or converted to public media by this procedure.

## Reviewer decision standard

Reviewers must approve only content that is respectful, culturally appropriate, privacy-preserving, and consistent with the member’s explicit consent. They must reject or return for private revision any content containing contact details, private family information, safety-related information, unapproved imagery, sensitive personal data, or claims that Bantabato guarantees a marriage outcome. Approved public wording should be measured and may use **“A Bantabato story has taken root.”**

## Withdrawal and records

Withdrawal immediately changes the normal-public visibility state. It does not erase the preceding consent, review, approval, publication, or withdrawal audit records required for accountability, policy, compliance, or incident review. The public listing is deliberately derived only from currently `published` records, so a withdrawn record disappears without deleting the controlled internal evidence.

## Staff separation and access

Editorial review and publication are separate roles. Review is permission-scoped; publication is fresh-authenticated, requires an approved independent operational record, and is blocked where the requester and approver are the same staff identity. Private review preview access is temporary and audited. No role receives broader member, family, safety, conversation, or verification access merely by reviewing editorial content.
