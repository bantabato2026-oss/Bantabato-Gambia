# Root / Trust-Line Motif Exploration

This exploration proposes a **future, subtle visual motif** for Bantabato; it is not a global implementation instruction. The motif would use a short forest-and-muted-gold line with one or two quiet nodes, reflecting patient growth and trustworthy boundaries rather than ranking, approval, or social status.

| Moment | Potential treatment | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo-adjacent moments | A quiet line beneath the existing official mark. | Never replace or compete with the official logo. |
| Section dividers | A low-contrast static divider between editorial public sections. | Preserve contrast, reading flow, and low-bandwidth performance. |
| Loading and success states | A static accent alongside existing semantic iconography. | Disable nonessential motion under reduced-motion and low-bandwidth preferences. |
| Connection moments | A private accent only after an already-authorized mutual connection. | Never disclose another member’s state, availability, or intent. |
| Family Circle | A boundary line within member-owned permission controls. | Never depict family membership, feedback, or relationships publicly. |
| Success stories | A restrained footer detail beneath voluntary approved editorial copy. | Never imply endorsement or expose private story sources, names, or media. |

The motif must never become a relationship graph, compatibility signal, verification score, family map, or safety indicator. Existing official branding and the current restrained visual system remain appropriate until a separate, accessible design decision approves a narrowly scoped implementation.
