## Privacy-preserving public presentation

The new public route does not turn a private declaration into a testimonial feed. It renders no content unless an actual member voluntarily completes the full consent and approval lifecycle. It does not expose private conversations, family context, verification materials, contact details, safety information, exact location, source drafts, storage keys, or original profile photos. Any future public photo presentation requires a separately approved media-derivative policy and must not reuse a private original path.

## Connector and deployment boundary

GitHub, Supabase, Sentry, and Cloudflare remain unactivated. No code has been pushed, no Supabase or Cloudflare resource changed, no member data transferred to Sentry, and no external monitoring or deployment claim made. No live payments, provider configuration, DNS action, or production operation was performed.

## Conservative completion conclusion

The current release closes the actual internal success-story gap without inflating product completion. It formalizes and verifies a voluntary, consent-first, reviewer-scoped, independently approved, and immediately withdrawable **text-only** public presentation path. The broader product remains **not launch-ready** and **not fully internally feature-complete** until the documented authenticated-accessibility, design-system, operational, provider, infrastructure, legal, and authorized test prerequisites are resolved.
